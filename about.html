Company file — Nu-Tech India

Over a decade of
advertising execution

Established in 2011 with its head office in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, Nu-Tech India has grown into one of the state's most trusted outdoor advertising and branding companies — built on in-house fabrication, a dedicated execution team, and work delivered for leading brands and government organizations.

Who we are

Built for statewide execution

Our expertise spans outdoor media, vehicle branding, wall paintings, LED signage, ACP cladding, exhibitions, product launches, roadshows, and integrated marketing activations.

Supported by advanced fabrication capabilities and a dedicated execution team, we ensure every project is delivered with quality, durability, and attention to detail — from the first site survey to final installation.

How we work

Professionalism, on schedule

At Nu-Tech India, customer satisfaction is our driving force. We believe in professionalism, transparency, timely execution, and long-term client relationships.

We're committed to serving clients round the clock, reachable by call, message, video call, or email — whether the brief is a single hoarding or a statewide rollout across all 30 districts.
